# Echo (working title)

A first-person horror game where you play as a blind person in your own house.

The screen is black. In this world, sound is what you see. When you click your tongue, a sound wave goes out from where you stand in every direction, and the room lights up as a field of dots as the wave reaches it, close things first. Close things are sharp and bright, far things are faint and blurry. That picture is a snapshot of the moment you clicked: you can turn your head and look all around inside it, but it doesn't follow you. It stays as long as you stand still and fades once you start walking.

The house has a living room, hallway, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, study and laundry room, full of furniture and everyday objects.

Almost everything sounds the same. Only **hollow** things (a door, a cabinet, a mug) and **glass** (windows, a TV screen, bottles) feel different.

And you are not alone. A man with a wooden right leg walks the house with a slight limp. You hear him (a shoe, then the hard knock of the peg) and feel his steps through the floor. When you click, the echo catches his silhouette, frozen wherever he was standing.

You make the echo with a tongue click. The house is never silent: a TV ad in the living room, the fan in the bedroom, a dripping tap in the bathroom, the washing machine in the laundry room, the fridge, the hallway clock. Whatever you can hear, you can faintly see: a weak, blurry shape where the sound comes from, even through walls, brighter when it's louder and dimmer when it's muffled behind a closed door. Only the echo shows things clearly.

Press Tab to switch to a sighted person's view and see the house as it really is.

## Play the prototype

1. Open Godot 4.5.1 and import this folder (`project.godot`).
2. Press **F5**. The main menu opens: **Play**, then **Tutorial** (you alone in the house, with hints) or **Level 1** (the house with the people in it).

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Space / left click | Echo (scan) |
| WASD | Walk (slow and quiet) |
| Shift | Run (fast and loud) |
| Mouse | Look around (inside the last echo snapshot) |
| E | Open/close a door, pick up / put down small objects, wind the alarm clock, turn the TV off/on |
| Tab | Switch between blind and sighted |
| E on a wardrobe / a bed | Hide inside / underneath (E again to get out) |
| E on the front door | Leave, if you hold the keys |
| Esc | Free the mouse; press again to go back to the menu |
