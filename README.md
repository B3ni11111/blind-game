# Echo (working title)

A first-person horror game where you play as a blind person in your own house.

The screen is black. When you make a sound, the echo paints the room as a field of dots, drawn top to bottom like a scanner. Close things are sharp and bright, far things are faint and blurry. That picture is frozen, like an afterimage: you can look around, but you keep seeing what you heard last. It stays as long as you stand still and fades once you start walking.

The house has a living room, hallway, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, study and laundry room, full of furniture and everyday objects.

Almost everything sounds the same. Only **hollow** things (a door, a cabinet, a mug) and **glass** (windows, a TV screen, bottles) feel different.

And you are not alone. A man with a wooden right leg walks the house with a slight limp. You hear him (a shoe, then the hard knock of the peg) and feel his steps through the floor. When you click, the echo catches his silhouette, frozen wherever he was standing.

You make the echo with a tongue click. The house is never silent: a TV ad in the living room, the fan in the bedroom, a dripping tap in the bathroom, the washing machine in the laundry room, the fridge, the hallway clock. You hear all of them from where they are (muffled behind closed doors), but you never see them.

Press Tab to switch to a sighted person's view and see the house as it really is.

## Play the prototype

1. Open Godot 4.5.1 and import this folder (`project.godot`).
2. Press **F5**.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Space / left click | Echo (scan) |
| WASD | Walk |
| Mouse | Turn (the frozen picture does not change) |
| E | Open/close a door, pick up / put down small objects |
| Tab | Switch between blind and sighted |
| Esc | Free the mouse |
